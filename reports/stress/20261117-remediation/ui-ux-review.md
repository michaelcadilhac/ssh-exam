## UX-08 follow-up — validation must be visible where the user is

**Confirmed rendered defect; corrected and tested locally.** The message had been moved out of Details, but submitting dates from the lower part of the narrow form left it above the content viewport. The [red screenshot](../../../var/stress-remediation/20261117/prof-rendered/date-validation-offscreen-mobile.png) shows no correction near the visible date fields/footer. [Geometry](../../../var/stress-remediation/20261117/prof-rendered/validation-geometry.json) places the alert at y = −106.66 to −55.47 while its scroll container begins at y = 131.75. There were zero preview requests. Ordinary scrolling revealed the [correct message](../../../var/stress-remediation/20261117/prof-rendered/date-validation-after-scroll-mobile.png). A DOM `toBeVisible` assertion alone had missed this defect.

The local correction focuses and scrolls the known validation alert after each failed draft submission. A ref guard prevents a closed dialog taking focus. The existing 375px two-screen/mixed-source regression now checks focus and full viewport visibility, scrolls away, submits the **same** invalid message again, and rechecks both. It retains zero previews for both failures, then continues the original source/settings/confirmation checks. Arbitrary server diagnostics and unconfirmed-operation reconciliation are unchanged.

The [focused run](../../../var/stress-remediation/20261117/prof-rendered/validation-focus-browser.log) passed one selected case in 10.2 seconds. Prof inspected the [fresh green screenshot](../../../var/stress-remediation/20261117/prof-rendered/validation-focus-browser/prof-another-attempt-anoth-0fca5--exact-per-student-settings/validation-visible-mobile.png): the corrective sentence is visible above the form. [Typecheck](../../../var/stress-remediation/20261117/prof-rendered/validation-focus-typecheck.log) and [build](../../../var/stress-remediation/20261117/prof-rendered/validation-focus-build.log) passed.
